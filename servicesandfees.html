    <h1>Services and Fees</h1>


    <h2>Services</h2>

    <p>I work with adults, teens (13+), and relational dyads.  I offer in-person sessions in Capitol Hill Seattle (near the Trader Joe’s at 17th & Madison) and online.</p>


    <h2>Fees</h2>

    <p>53 minute individual therapy session - $190 </p>

    <p>75 minute relational/couples therapy session - $240 </p>

    <p>90 minute relational/couples therapy session - $290 </p>

    <p>Payment is due in full at the time of service.  I accept credit, debit, and HSA/FSA.</p>

    <p>I believe that everyone should have access to therapy.  I offer limited sliding scale slots that are available for those with low incomes and other financial constraints.  I encourage you to contact me to discuss an adjusted rate if you can’t pay the full fee.</p>

    <p>As described <a href="./Resources/NoSurpriseBilling.pdf" target="_blank"><strong>here</strong></a>, you have the right to receive a “good faith estimate” for the cost of my services, which I will provide to you if we work together.</p>


    <h2>Insurance</h2>

    <p>I‘m not in network with insurance providers. However, I can provide you with invoices (also known as “superbills”) that you can send to your insurance company to request reimbursement. </p>

    <p>If you are interested in working together and seeking reimbursement from your insurance company for my services, I recommend asking your insurance company the following questions: </p>

    <ul class="questions">

      <li>— Do I have out-of-network benefits? And if I do:</li>

      <li>— Does my plan cover reimbursement for services from Licensed Mental Health Counselors  (LMHC’s) for CPT code 90837 (individual) or 90847 (relational)?</li>

      <li>— Does my plan limit how many sessions per calendar year I can have?</li>

      <li>— What is the percentage of reimbursement for out-of-network services? </li>

      <li>— Do I have to meet my deductible before reimbursement occurs?  If so, how far away am I from that?</li>

    </ul>
